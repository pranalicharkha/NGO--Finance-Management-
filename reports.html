Nidigo Transparency Commitment
Financial Integrity and Accountability Statement
All financial transactions recorded in this system are maintained in line with NGO transparency and accountability standards. Every income entry is linked to a verifiable source and every expense is categorized for clear audit traceability.

This ledger can be reviewed by stakeholders, registered donors, partner organizations, and regulatory authorities upon request. Nidigo is committed to ensuring that every rupee received is accounted for and every expenditure is justified against organizational objectives.

Data integrity is maintained through periodic internal audits. All records are timestamped and stored securely.
