Nidigo Transparency Commitment
Financial Integrity & Accountability Statement
All financial transactions recorded in this system are maintained in strict accordance with NGO transparency and accountability standards. Every income entry is linked to a verifiable source — whether a donor, grant body, or government authority — and every expense is categorized for clear audit traceability.

This ledger is available for review by stakeholders, registered donors, partner organizations, and regulatory authorities upon formal request. Nidigo is committed to ensuring that every rupee received is accounted for and every expenditure is justified against organizational objectives.

Data integrity is maintained through periodic internal audits. All records are timestamped and stored securely. Unauthorized modification of financial records is strictly prohibited and subject to organizational disciplinary action.
