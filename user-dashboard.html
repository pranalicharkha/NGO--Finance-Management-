About Nidigo Foundation
Our mission & vision

Nidigo Foundation is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to improving the lives of underprivileged communities through sustainable development programs. Since our establishment in 2018, we have impacted over 5,000 lives across education, healthcare, clean water, and skill development initiatives.

Our work is guided by transparency, accountability, and measurable impact. Every donation received is carefully tracked and allocated to specific projects. Donors have full visibility into how their contributions are being utilized through our comprehensive reporting system.
